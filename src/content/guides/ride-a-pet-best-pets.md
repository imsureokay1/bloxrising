Ranking pets by how rare they look gets you the wrong answer. The pet with the best income isn't the fastest, the highest jump doesn't belong to the rarest pet, and one of the top ten has worse odds than the pet above it. This list uses the actual stats.

| 1 | Kitsune | 1 in 1 quintillion | 1,000,000,000 | 170 | 90,000,000 |

| 10 | Lion | 1 in 1.2 million | 6,520 | 120 | 510 |

## 1. Kitsune

![Kitsune in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/kitsune.webp)

The best pet overall. A billion base speed is more than three times Dragon's, and its 170 jump is the highest in the game. If you want to move and progress, nothing else comes close.

Oddly, its 1 in 1 quintillion hatch chance is *better* odds than Dragon's, despite being the stronger pet for everything except income.

## 2. Dragon

![Dragon in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/dragon.webp)

The money king. 300 million income is the highest of any pet, triple Kitsune's, so if your goal is earning rather than moving, Dragon wins outright. It's still the second-fastest pet in the game.

## 3. Cerberus

![Cerberus in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/cerberus.webp)

The biggest single jump on the list. Cerberus runs at 100 million speed against Phoenix's 1 million, so getting one changes how the whole map feels. Income is strong too at 30 million.

## 4. Phoenix

![Phoenix in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/phoenix.webp)

Where the end game starts. Nearly four times T-Rex's speed, and its 146 jump is among the best in the game, which makes it comfortable to use as well as fast.

## 5. T-Rex

![T-Rex in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/t-rex.webp)

Beats Unicorn on the numbers that matter: almost double the speed and better income. Its 100 jump is the one place Unicorn wins, so if you're choosing between them for progression, take T-Rex.

## 6. Unicorn

![Unicorn in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/unicorn.webp)

The first pet where the stats stop looking normal. 137,000 speed and 30,000 income is a huge leap over Cheetah, and 135 jump makes it pleasant to ride.

## 7. Cheetah

![Cheetah in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/cheetah.webp)

The best pet before the end-game tier, and the first one whose name matches its performance. 38,400 speed with a solid 117 jump.

## 8. Giraffe

![Giraffe in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/giraffe.webp)

The worst jump in the top ten at 83, which does make it clumsy. The speed and income gains over Fox are large enough that it's still worth using.

## 9. Fox

![Fox in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/fox.webp)

Where the game starts feeling genuinely fast. 19,500 speed is a massive step up from Lion, and 1,200 income more than doubles it.

## 10. Lion

![Lion in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/lion.webp)

Ostrich is slightly faster and earns a bit more, but Lion's 120 jump against Ostrich's 86 makes it the more comfortable pet to actually play with, which is why it takes the last spot.

## Don't waste mutations on pets you'll replace

The most common mistake is pouring mutation resources into an early or mid-game pet. You'll drop it within the hour.

Use the fastest pet you have, improve your route, reach better eggs, and save heavy investment for a pet you'll actually keep, which realistically means Phoenix or above.
