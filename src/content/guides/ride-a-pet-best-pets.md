In Ride A Pet, a pet's rarity and its usefulness don't line up neatly. Kitsune is easier to hatch than Dragon but moves over three times faster, and Dragon is the one to pick if you only care about money. This list orders the top 10 by the stats community wikis list for each pet, and says what each one is actually good for.

**About the numbers:** every stat and hatch chance below comes from the community wikis linked above. We haven't checked them in-game. Sources also describe the income stat differently, as either a cash rate or a bonus, so treat it as a comparison between pets rather than an exact payout.

## The top 10 at a glance

| # | Pet | Hatch chance | Speed | Jump | Income |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kitsune | 1 in 1 quintillion | 1,000,000,000 | 170 | 90,000,000 |
| 2 | Dragon | 1 in 5 quintillion | 300,000,000 | 150 | 300,000,000 |
| 3 | Cerberus | 1 in 70 quadrillion | 100,000,000 | 143 | 30,000,000 |
| 4 | Phoenix | 1 in 30 trillion | 1,000,000 | 146 | 100,000 |
| 5 | T-Rex | 1 in 10 trillion | 266,000 | 100 | 50,000 |
| 6 | Unicorn | 1 in 1 trillion | 137,000 | 135 | 30,000 |
| 7 | Cheetah | 1 in 7 billion | 38,400 | 117 | 3,500 |
| 8 | Giraffe | 1 in 300 million | 27,400 | 83 | 2,200 |
| 9 | Fox | 1 in 21 million | 19,500 | 105 | 1,200 |
| 10 | Lion | 1 in 1.2 million | 6,520 | 120 | 510 |

## 1. Kitsune

![Kitsune in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/kitsune.webp)

The best all-rounder. Its listed billion speed is more than three times Dragon's, and its 170 jump is the highest listed for any pet. If you want to move around the map and reach far eggs, nothing else is close.

Its listed hatch chance, 1 in 1 quintillion, is actually better odds than Dragon's.

## 2. Dragon

![Dragon in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/dragon.webp)

The income pick. Its listed income is the highest of any pet and about triple Kitsune's, so if earning matters more than moving, Dragon wins. It's still listed as the second-fastest pet.

## 3. Cerberus

![Cerberus in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/cerberus.webp)

The biggest step up on the list. Cerberus is listed at 100 million speed against Phoenix's 1 million, so on paper getting one changes how the whole map feels. Income is strong too.

## 4. Phoenix

![Phoenix in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/phoenix.webp)

Where the end game starts. Nearly four times T-Rex's listed speed and a 146 jump. Community guides also point out that it flies, which helps with distant egg spawns.

## 5. T-Rex

![T-Rex in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/t-rex.webp)

Beats Unicorn on speed and income. Unicorn only wins on jump, so if you're choosing between them for progression, T-Rex is the better pick on these numbers.

## 6. Unicorn

![Unicorn in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/unicorn.webp)

A big jump over Cheetah in both speed and income, with a solid 135 jump.

## 7. Cheetah

![Cheetah in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/cheetah.webp)

The strongest pet below the end-game group, with 38,400 listed speed and a 117 jump.

## 8. Giraffe

![Giraffe in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/giraffe.webp)

The lowest jump in the top 10 at 83. Its speed and income are still well ahead of Fox.

## 9. Fox

![Fox in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/fox.webp)

The first big speed step, roughly three times Lion's listed speed, with more than double its income.

## 10. Lion

![Lion in Ride A Pet](/images/ride-a-pet/pets/lion.webp)

Ostrich is listed as slightly faster with slightly more income, but Lion's 120 jump against Ostrich's 86 makes it easier to get around with, which is why it takes the last spot here.

## Don't spend mutations on pets you'll replace

Mutation resources are limited, and early or mid-game pets get replaced quickly. Save heavy investment for a pet you'll actually keep, which on these numbers means Phoenix or above.
