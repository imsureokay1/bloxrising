Ride A Pet looks simple, but the gap between a new player and a good one comes down to a few habits. Here's what actually speeds you up in your first few hours.

## Grab the free stuff first

Before anything else:

- **Claim your Index rewards.** You get a payout every five pets you collect, and the first one is worth roughly 50,000 to 100,000 cash. That alone can buy you a Royal Radar on day one.
- **Join the event at spawn.** Look for the money bag near the statue of the bacon riding the phoenix. Joining gives you a free 2x money boost, and joining during the admin abuse event gives three hours of doubled cash.
- **Join the group and like the game** for free meat every day. Meat is one of the better foods in the game, so this is a daily freebie worth taking.

## Feed your pets

Food is the most underrated upgrade. Feeding is a straight multiplier on your pet's speed, and it costs far less than chasing a better pet.

A handful of bones (around 50,000 XP) can add tens of millions of speed to a strong pet, and a fully fed high-tier pet can end up roughly double what it started at. If you're choosing between saving for a new pet and feeding the one you have, feed first.

## Weather mutations are the real shortcut

When the time of day flips, there's a chance a weather event rolls in. Weathers can strike both **your pets** and **every egg on the map**, permanently mutating them.

- A Rage mutation makes a pet around four times better in speed and cash.
- Eternal is the jackpot, worth about a hundred times the money, and it's rarer than the rarest egg in the game.
- Even a common pet with a good mutation can outperform a legendary one without it.

Two things to do whenever a weather is active:

1. **Go steal the best egg you can reach**, since it'll carry the mutation.
2. **Stay logged in and idle** so your own pets have a chance to get struck.

## Stealing eggs: timers beat speed

Taking an egg starts a break timer, either 20 or 30 seconds depending on the egg. That's the real limit, not your pet's speed. A slow pet with only about a million speed can still bring back a top-tier egg if the spawn is close by.

What matters is knowing where the rare eggs appear.

### Black hole and Troop egg spawn locations

- **The waterfall**, including inside the secret room and behind the falls. Three separate spots, and the easiest area to reach.
- **The tree beside the waterfall.**
- **The hillside and mountain** above the waterfall area.
- **The beach temple**, by the water cave wall near the pyramid.
- **The big tree**, anywhere on the trunk, inside it, or out on the branches. This is the furthest spot, so a flying pet helps.

One of these eggs appears roughly every 5 to 10 minutes, and the spot changes each time eggs reset. So if a reset happens mid-search, check the places you already looked.

## Best mount for getting around

The Phoenix is worth using even over higher-tier pets, because flying gets you to distant spawns in seconds. Reach matters more than raw stats when you're racing a timer.

## Rebirths, money and luck

Money feels unimportant early, then becomes the whole game. Luck upgrades cost cash, and luck decides what you actually get out of a rare egg. The rarity gaps between top pets are enormous, so without luck upgrades you can steal the best egg in the game and still open something common.

So the loop later on is: rebirth, upgrade luck, use mutated pets to earn more, then repeat. Mutations are the biggest money multiplier you have.

## Do you need the paid radar?

The infinite radar shows every egg on the map and is genuinely convenient, but it's a shortcut, not a requirement. Learn the spawn spots above and you can find the same eggs for free.

## Avoid scripts

You'll see "auto egg farm" and "free cash" scripts advertised for this game. They're exploits, they break Roblox's rules, and they can get your account permanently banned. Nothing in Ride A Pet is worth losing your account over.
